**External images:** Do not assume that every ESA, NASA or Copernicus webpage image is public domain or Creative Commons. Review the individual image credit, photographer and licence before downloading and redistributing it. An educational reuse permission may not authorise unrestricted commercial reuse. Do not imply institutional endorsement.

**Current image hosting:** The three photographs/satellite images above are embedded directly from Wikimedia Commons, with the licence and original source linked. Their binary files have **not** yet been copied into this repository. The editable original SVG diagrams are held locally under `images/`.
