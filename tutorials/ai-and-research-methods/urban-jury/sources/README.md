# Tempelhofer Feld — shared starting evidence

This folder provides **the same initial research basis** for every student following [The Contested Public Space](../README.md). It was prepared on **14 November 2026** from the three named official sources. The notes are original, carefully attributed **paraphrases of publicly available information**, **not reproductions of the publishers' original webpages**, and should not be confused with verified quotations or a complete planning record.

## Why we do not host the three webpages in full

The Tempelhofer Feld website, published by Grün Berlin GmbH, states in its [legal notice](https://www.tempelhoferfeld.de/impressum/) that its content is subject to German copyright and that reproduction and distribution outside statutory exceptions require permission. Berlin.de content also has rights and re-use conditions that depend on the material. Merely being publicly accessible does not mean we may redistribute a complete copy in a public repository.

Instead we commit:
- [source-register.json](source-register.json): stable source IDs, original URLs, publishers, original language, access date and limitations.
- [evidence-notes.md](evidence-notes.md): fixed, independently authored fact notes with claim IDs and precise source locations.
- [evidence-notes.json](evidence-notes.json): the same notes in a simple machine-readable structure for the coding exercises.

**These are study notes, not original evidence passages.** When Chapter 5 asks the application to verify a claim against source passages, retrieve/inspect the **original linked documents** yourself, record the exact document or permitted extract used and cite the appropriate location. The prewritten notes can initialise a shared context but must not be labelled as verbatim extracts or independent verification.

## Use in the tutorial

1. Begin with the same [evidence notes](evidence-notes.md), so agents have a fixed common background.
2. Keep this folder's Git commit SHA in your experiment log so other students can identify which version you used.
3. Follow source IDs **S001–S003** and claim IDs **E001–E012**. Preserve the links between claims and source sections.
4. Verify important claims against the linked original pages. If you save any local originals or extracts, record their retrieval date, page/section location and file hash in **your own** experiment log.
5. Treat questions about contemporary conditions, public opinion or ecological measurements as unresolved unless additional appropriate evidence is supplied.

**Important limitation:** This three-source collection is institutional, incomplete and not representative of Berlin residents. It does not provide a complete legal, ecological or housing feasibility study, nor the perspectives of local campaigns or all participants. Sources can change after the recorded access date.

No copyrighted images or complete copies of the three websites are included.
