# Tempelhofer Feld — fixed evidence notes

*Prepared 14 November 2026 from the three official webpages listed in [source-register.json](source-register.json). This is original paraphrased teaching material, **not the source text** and not a complete or independent verification of claims. Follow source links to inspect the original pages.*

## S001 — About Tempelhofer Feld

Original: https://www.tempelhoferfeld.de/en/pflege-entwicklung/about-tempelhofer-feld/  
Publisher: Grün Berlin GmbH / Tempelhofer Feld  
Relevant headings: **About Tempelhofer Feld**; **An urban open space with a multi-layered history**

- **E001.** Tempelhofer Feld has been open for public recreation since 2010, with more than 300 hectares available for leisure activities and nature. **Source:** S001, About Tempelhofer Feld.
- **E002.** The site supports protected plants and rare animal species, as well as recreation and civic initiatives. **Source:** S001, About Tempelhofer Feld.
- **E003.** The site's recorded history includes aviation, Nazi-era persecution and forced labour, and the Berlin Airlift of 1948–49. **Source:** S001, An urban open space with a multi-layered history.
- **E004.** Airport operations ceased in 2008; the former airfield opened to the public in 2010. **Source:** S001, An urban open space with a multi-layered history.
- **E005.** The official overview describes the 2014 vote as rejecting development at the edges and supporting preservation of the field, followed by a preservation law in June 2014. **Source:** S001, An urban open space with a multi-layered history.

## S002 — Participation Model

Original: https://www.tempelhoferfeld.de/en/participate-get-involved/participation-model/  
Publisher: Grün Berlin GmbH / Tempelhofer Feld  
Relevant heading: **Participation Model**

- **E006.** The Law for the Preservation of Tempelhofer Feld (ThFG) came into force in June 2014 following the referendum. **Source:** S002, Participation Model.
- **E007.** Citizens, the relevant Senate administration and Grün Berlin jointly developed a development and maintenance plan (*Entwicklungs- und Pflegeplan*, EPP) during 2014–2016. **Source:** S002, Participation Model.
- **E008.** The EPP took effect in May 2016 and addresses nature, history, recreation and civic engagement. **Source:** S002, Participation Model.
- **E009.** A continuing participation model involves administration, Grün Berlin and elected citizens in the implementation of measures for the field. **Source:** S002, Participation Model.

## S003 — Berlin Senate dialogue process

Original: https://www.berlin.de/sen/stadtentwicklung/staedtebau/einzelprojekte/tempelhofer-feld/  
Publisher: Berlin Senate Department for Urban Development, Building and Housing  
Language: German. Notes below are English paraphrases.  
Relevant headings: **Dialogprozess Tempelhofer Feld**; **Fragen und Antworten**

- **E010.** The Senate describes an international ideas competition and public dialogue aimed at informing debate about the field's future, explicitly distinguishing that process from a decision to build on its perimeter. **Source:** S003, introductory text under Dialogprozess Tempelhofer Feld.
- **E011.** The Senate frames the dialogue in relation to housing demand, social infrastructure such as schools and day-care provision, climate change and transport transition. **Source:** S003, introductory text and Dialogprozess Tempelhofer Feld.
- **E012.** The Senate's description of the dialogue process includes workshops involving Berlin residents selected by a random sampling process and an international ideas competition; it presents these as contributions to a broader public debate rather than a substitute for it. **Source:** S003, Dialogprozess Tempelhofer Feld and Fragen und Antworten.
